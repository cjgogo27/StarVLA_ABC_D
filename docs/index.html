Core diagnosis

Progress awareness is the missing signal: push tasks change the scene gradually, so the policy can touch the block but still fail to know whether the subtask has advanced enough.

Early-chain sensitivityFailure at SR@1 or SR@2 sharply lowers average chain length and prevents later subtasks.
Push ambiguityPush-left/right remain low even in the best MoT run, confirming that progress cues matter.
Remedy pathHistory context, progress prediction, future-frame conditioning, and RL rewards all target progress estimation.
